# NYX Engineering Workflow

## Complete Lifecycle
1. **Problem Report**: User submits short natural-language description.
2. **Triage**: Team Lead inspects the issue and determines required specialist roles.
3. **Reproduction**: QA / Engineering attempts to reproduce the defect and isolate root cause.
4. **Root-Cause Analysis**: Backend/Security/Crypto specialists investigate codebase.
5. **Task Delegation**: Team Lead assigns implementation/review tasks.
6. **Implementation**: Minimal, maintainable patch applied following architectural rules.
7. **Independent Review**: Security/crypto/architecture review where applicable.
8. **Focused Testing**: Regression tests executed and observed.
9. **Regression Verification**: Final sanity check and diff review.
10. **Final Report**: Concise summary delivered to user.
