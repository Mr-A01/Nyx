Capabilities

Engineered for trust,
not just features.

Every architectural decision in NYX is grounded in a single principle: the user owns their identity, their keys, and their communication. Nothing else is negotiable.

Identity Ownership

Your .nyxid file is encrypted with a key derived only from your password. No backdoor, no recovery key held by anyone else. Lose the password, lose the file — that's the design.

Federated Relays

No central server. Messages route through independent relay nodes operated by the community. Each relay publishes its health, and the observatory verifies it.

Observable Health

Every relay exposes a public health endpoint. Latency, uptime, federation links — all verifiable by anyone. No trust required, only evidence.

Strong Cryptography

Argon2id for key derivation, XChaCha20-Poly1305 for authenticated encryption, TOTP (RFC 6238) for second factor. No homegrown algorithms. No shortcuts.

Idempotent Delivery

Every outgoing message carries an idempotency key. Retry safely. Reconnect without duplicates. The protocol guarantees exactly-once semantics at the relay layer.

Terminal-Native

Built for engineers first. Keyboard shortcuts, command palettes, monospace metadata, dense information display. Every interaction respects your time.

Architecture

A distributed system,
by design.

NYX is not a client-server application. It's a federation of independent relay nodes, each operated by a different party, communicating through a well-defined protocol.

Client

Messenger, Identity, Observatory — standalone applications sharing the NYX design language and protocol.

Relay Node

Independent PHP servers publishing health endpoints, routing messages, maintaining federation links.

Federation

Peer discovery, heartbeat-based health, malicious relay detection, reputation scoring — all verifiable.
